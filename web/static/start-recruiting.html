The recruiting draft runs week by week, one week per roster spot, each as long as the commissioner set. In any week, any team can bid on any player - search the whole pool (with last season's points and school), place a bid, and raise or retract it whenever you like. Every player has a hidden price for the whole recruitment (the average player costs $10 a week, so $200 over 20 weeks). When a week ends, each player whose bids add up to his price goes to the highest bidder, and every team that bid on him pays its bid - even the teams that didn't win him. The rest carry into the next week, uncharged. After the final week, the highest bidder gets any player still standing. Each week ends with a 20-second pause - no bids can be placed - and a results screen showing how each of your bids came out and how close each player's recruitment is to completing. Your budget is weekly and accumulates (Large $300, Moderate $200, Small $100 a week - skip week 1 as a Large program and you have $600 in week 2). You can't bid more than the money you have - your standing bids all count against it. AI-owned teams bid on their own.
