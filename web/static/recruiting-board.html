Bid on any player, any time. Every player has a hidden price for the whole recruitment. When a week ends, each player whose bids add up to his price goes to the highest bidder - and every team that bid on him pays its bid, even if it didn't win him. Bids on players who aren't awarded carry into the next week, uncharged. Your budget is weekly and accumulates, and you can't bid more than you have: your standing bids all count against your money, so lower or retract one to free it up. Points are last season's (last season) fantasy points, ESPN standard scoring.
